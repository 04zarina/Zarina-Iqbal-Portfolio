## About Me
Hi there! I am Alex Morgan, a passionate game programmer, technical artist, and web 3D developer. I blend procedural generation, tactile physics, and cozy aesthetics to build games and web toys that bring smiles.

I have spent the last 4 years studying Computer Science with an emphasis on Computer Graphics and Game Architecture. Whether rigging a low-poly character in Blender, writing custom shaders in GLSL / HLSL, or optimizing Three.js web render loops to run at a crisp 60 FPS on mobile phones, I love making the virtual world feel tangible and responsive.

## Interests
- **Cozy Game Design:** Mechanics centered around tactile joy, gardening, cooking, and soft exploration.
- **Real-Time Graphics:** Shader programming, stylized non-photorealistic rendering (NPR), and performant web 3D.
- **Physical Computing & Tools:** Building custom level editors, DCC plugins, and micro-tools for teammates.
- **Sound & Atmosphere:** Lo-fi music production, Foley sound effects, and ambient generative soundscapes.

## Goals
- Create emotionally resonant indie titles with small, dedicated creative teams.
- Push the boundaries of 3D interactive storytelling in the browser using WebGL and WebGPU.
- Collaborate with animators, artists, and sound designers to breathe life into playful virtual diorama worlds.
